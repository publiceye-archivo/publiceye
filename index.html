About

We keep the record of how style was made, worn, and remembered.

Publiceye is an independent archive of fashion, film, and design. We collect the artifacts that ordinary histories overlook — the labels, the contact sheets, the press clippings, the reels — and we restore them so they can be seen again.

What began as a private collection of garments and stills has grown into a catalogued, decade-by-decade record open to researchers, designers, and the merely curious.
